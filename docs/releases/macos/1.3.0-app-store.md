## Description

Routina is a flexible planner for routines, todos, focused work, and meaningful progress. It keeps daily planning practical while giving you detailed control when you need it.

PLAN YOUR DAY

• See today’s work, upcoming tasks, and overdue items in one organized workspace
• Arrange work across Day, 3 Days, or Week calendar views
• Use the chronological timeline to review plans and completed activity
• Add planned dates, time blocks, estimates, and availability windows

BUILD ROUTINES THAT MATCH REAL LIFE

• Create one-time todos or reusable repeating routines
• Repeat after completion or follow daily, weekly, monthly, and advanced schedules
• Choose Due behavior for firm commitments or Gentle behavior for lower-pressure routines
• Use standard completion or flexible checklists

FOCUS WITH CONTEXT

• Start a focus session for a task or group of tagged tasks
• Pause and resume without counting break time
• Review recent sessions and focused time alongside task history

UNDERSTAND YOUR PROGRESS

• Explore customizable statistics for today, week, month, year, or any date range
• Review completions, active days, focus time, routines, and open todos
• Filter your statistics to see the progress that matters to you

ORGANIZE YOUR WAY

Use tags, priorities, importance, urgency, custom sections, links, files, images, comments, and history to give every task the context it needs. Search quickly, keep important details close at hand, and shape the workspace around how you plan.

Designed for macOS, Routina combines a spacious interface, Dark Mode, flexible sidebars, calendar planning, iCloud sync, and backup import and export.
